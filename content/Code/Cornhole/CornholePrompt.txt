2. The top section will be red and will have the text "Team 1". At the top of the section will be two buttons. One button says "+1" and another button says "+3". Under the buttons will be two text boxes. One will be labeled "Current Round" and the other will be labeled "Overall Score". Both text boxes will be initialized to 0 and will not be editable by the user at any point.

3. The bottom section will consist of the same elements as the top section, but will have the text "Team 2" instead of "Team 1", and be blue instead of red.

4. In the center of the page, overlapping the top and bottom sections will be a button that says "End Round".

5. At the bottom of the page will be a button that says "Restart Game"

The site will be used as follows:
Team 1 and Team 2 will take turns playing. The user will press the "+1" and/or "+3" button to increase each Team's score during their turn. Once a round is completed, the user will press the "End Round" button. At this point the overall score for each player at the end of the round is calculated. The score is calculated by subtracting the lowest score from the highest score, and the remaining points are awarded to the Team with the highest score for the round. For example, if Team 1 has 4 points and Team 2 has 6 points, the score at the end of the round is 0 for Team 1 and 2 for Team 2. Once the scores are calculated, the value in the "Overall Score" text box is increased for each player by the amount they scored in the particular round. Then the value in the "Current Round" is set to 0.

If the user presses the "Restart Game" button, then the value for the "Current Round" and the "Overall Score" text boxes will be reset to 0.

Additional requirements:
This website will most likely be used on a phone, so make sure the layout will fit well when the phone is held in portrait mode.
