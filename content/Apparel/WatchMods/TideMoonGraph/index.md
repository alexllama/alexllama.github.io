Continuing with my obsession with modifying cheap Casio digital watches, I found this one at Walmart. 
{{< figure src="tmgwalmart.jpg" caption="What a steal!" >}}
{{< figure src="tmgorig.jpg" caption="Casio loves printing words on their watches" >}}

After getting it home I realized that most of the lettering on the case was embossed, so I would not be able to erase it with my trusty white eraser. I did get the words on the top and bottom to come off, but they left behind a shadow of the lettering. 
{{< figure src="tmgfinal.jpg" caption="Slightly less words on the case" >}}

When I opened it I realized the faceplate doesn't come off like it does on the [Casio Royale](/apparel/watchmods/casioroyale), so I was not going to be able to remove that lettering either...dissapointing!

In the end I tried a couple of different colored gels, but none looked that great. Here are a couple of attempts
