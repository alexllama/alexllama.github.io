I found this website [The Printable Watch](https://theprintablewatch.com/) that offers files you can download and print your own watch case. I thought this was the coolest idea, and bought a whole kit. 

I don't have a 3D printer, so I hired [Connor The Maker](https://www.youtube.com/@ConnorTheMaker) to print it for me. I had met him at the Rochester Maker Faire, where he was displaying projects he had made. He was a student at the Rochester Institute of Technology and had started this 3D printing side business, so I was happy to help out a young engineering student.

Assembling it was a bit of a pain. Working with such small watch hands is quite challenging, even when you have all the right tools, which thankfully came with the kit I ordered. 

I really like how it came out, all white with a black chapter ring. plus the fact that it is fully 3D printed adds to the cool factor!
