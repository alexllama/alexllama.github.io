Elsewhere on this site you will find a [clock](/electronics/cigarboxclock) I made with a cigar box. After getting into 3D Printing and finding so many existing projects out there I had to make a new clock. This one is of an Omega Speedmaster chronograph. It uses the Bambu Lab clock kit, which includes a second hand that sweeps like a mechanical watch. The quality is amazing, though the chronograph hands are just superglued to the dial.
