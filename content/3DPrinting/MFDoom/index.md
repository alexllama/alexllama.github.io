When I got the 3D printer for Christmas 2025 I proudly proclaimed that I would only use it for things that were useful, and I would not waste precious filament on knick knacks to clutter my desk. That lasted about a month, until I ran into this MF Doom model. I wanted it in color but didnt have any colors other than black and white. So I just printed it in white and painted it whith acrylic paints.
